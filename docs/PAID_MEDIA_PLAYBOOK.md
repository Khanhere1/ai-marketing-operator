# Paid Media Advisory and Execution Playbook

## Objective

Connect media activity to qualified demand, revenue, margin, and capacity while preserving human control over consequential spend and platform changes.

## Standard review

1. Business objective and economics
2. Measurement and attribution audit
3. Platform-level analysis
4. CRM/order and margin reconciliation
5. Constraint diagnosis
6. Alternatives and expected mechanisms
7. Immediate task sequence
8. Long-term capability sequence
9. Media-buyer handoff
10. Outcome review and qualified learning

## Immediate operator sequence template

- Verify conversion, attribution, currency, timezone, and lead-quality definitions.
- Review high-spend segments with poor downstream outcomes.
- Identify irrelevant queries, placements, audiences, or creative fatigue.
- Check message, offer, and landing-page alignment.
- Prepare exact reversible change sets.
- Define primary/guardrail metrics and observation window.
- Obtain transaction approval where required.
- Record actual manual or automated changes.
- Review results without overstating causality.

## Long sequence template

- Repair offline conversion and CRM feedback loops.
- Establish naming, campaign, audience, and creative taxonomies.
- Build asset/claim registry.
- Implement experiment registry.
- Develop margin and diminishing-return models.
- Plan holdouts or incrementality tests where feasible.
- Qualify narrow actions for bounded execution.

## Required distinctions

- Platform-reported conversions
- CRM/transaction-observed business outcomes
- Experimentally estimated incremental outcomes
