# AI Marketing Maturity Model

## Stage 0 — Foundation and readiness
Context, metric definitions, data quality, brand claims, access, consent, policy, baselines, owners.

## Stage 1 — AI-assisted production
Research, reporting, briefs, drafts, basic QA, human-controlled execution, CAC/CPA/ROAS and business-outcome fundamentals.

## Stage 2 — Growth and experimentation
Hypothesis/test/iteration, VoC at scale, SEO/AEO/GEO testing, cross-channel intelligence, experiment registry and learning.

## Stage 3 — Marketing operations and organizational scale
Adoption, playbooks, tooling economics, sales-marketing SLAs, governance, workflow qualification, executive impact reporting.

## Stage 4 — AI-native GTM and governed autonomy
Portfolio planning, incrementality, cost curves, qualified bounded actions, closed-loop revenue intelligence, strategic adaptation.

Assess each capability dimension separately; organizations may be advanced in content and immature in measurement.
